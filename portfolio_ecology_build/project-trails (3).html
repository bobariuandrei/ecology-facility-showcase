Trails

A one-off visual collaboration for an local scene party series. Full creative direction — concept, image, and original poetry. The work was printed as postcards and hidden across the venue for guests to discover throughout the night, like an egg hunt. The poem was written for the event and printed across the postcards.

RoleVisual Direction · Concept · Text
FormatPrint — Postcard Series
TypeOne-off Collaboration
ContextUnderground Party Series, Bucharest
From the postcards
the devouring woman has started to devour
she painted her face red
she's ready now
for both me and you
must run (calmly)
or hide (in plain sight)
or spread ur legs (shamefully)
else she'll enter your soul
here comes hunger
here comes wrath
here comes pain
can't wait no more
