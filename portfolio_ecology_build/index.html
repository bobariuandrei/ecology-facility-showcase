Contact

Six years. Fifteen exhibitions. Three publications. A documentary. A collective built from zero that clients keep coming back to.

I don't pick one medium because the work doesn't ask me to. Photography, film, painting, writing, design — each one is a different pressure on the same material. I come from psychology and trauma therapy, which means I understand what moves people before I start moving things around on a page.

Based in Bucharest. Available internationally. Interested in the work that actually matters.
